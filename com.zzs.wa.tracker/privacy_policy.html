2. Information We Collect

We follow a data-minimization principle and collect only the information necessary to provide the App and its features.

  • Device Information: information about the mobile device you use, such as the device model, unique device identifier, IP address, mobile operating system version, and browser type.
  • Usage Data: information about how you interact with the App, such as the features you access and session duration.
  • Billing Information: if you make a purchase, payment information is collected and processed by Google Play Billing. Dopzzs LLC does not store your credit card or payment account details on our servers.
  • Input Data: the phone numbers and keywords that you enter in the App for monitoring are processed only to deliver the tracking feature you requested. We treat this data as confidential.
  • AI Interaction Data: when you use AI-powered features such as AI analysis reports, the prompts and activity data you submit are transmitted to Google's servers and processed by generative AI models (Gemini) through Firebase AI Logic. Google processes this data in accordance with its own privacy policies. We do not use your AI interaction data for any purpose other than providing the AI feature to you.

Google's use of data transmitted to these services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, How Google Uses Information From Partner & Developer Sites That Use Google Services, and the Firebase Terms of Service.
